FP&A Web App — Wireframe

Desktop 1440 · near-final layout

All eleven screens from sitemap.md, plus six states beyond the happy path. Structure follows design.md §3 and the step-by-step flows in the userflow doc. Figures are placeholders in the real shapes the calculation engine returns; territories, P&L line labels and dataset names are the actual ones in the model.

The charts carry over from the twelve Stitch mockups, re-cut in this system: bars and bridges built from hairline rules and two steps of the steel accent rather than a second colour. Throughout, solid steel worsens a result and light steel offsets it — one convention, used on every chart.
